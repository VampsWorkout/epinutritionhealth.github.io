Understanding Your Health Indicators

Our comprehensive approach tracks three key metrics to provide a complete picture of your metabolic health

🥦

Nutritional Score

The Nutritional Score evaluates the quality of your diet based on your food choices. It considers factors like nutrient density, macronutrient balance, and processed food intake to help you understand how well your nutrition supports your body's needs.

Optimal Diet Quality Key to Performance

A higher score means your diet is rich in whole, nutrient-dense foods that fuel optimal health.

🔥

Metabolic Score

The Metabolic Score measures how efficiently your body processes energy. It takes into account key health markers such as blood sugar stability, insulin sensitivity, and energy levels throughout the day.

Energy Efficiency Sustained Performance

A higher score indicates a well-functioning metabolism, essential for maintaining steady energy, weight balance, and long-term health.

⏳

Metabolic Age

Your Metabolic Age reflects how your body's metabolism compares to an average person of your chronological age. It provides a tangible measure of your metabolic health relative to population norms.

Biological Optimization -8 Years

A lower metabolic age than your chronological age indicates your body functions more efficiently than the norm.
